ML and advanced analytics

Go beyond what one query can answer.

For questions that need statistical reasoning, Jeen proposes a governed analysis from the methods enabled in your deployment. You review or adjust the setup before it runs, then get visual results, confidence information, caveats, and inspectable model details.

  • Forecast and monitor change over time
  • Explain patterns and quantify relationships
  • Segment entities and evaluate outcomes
Predict and monitor

Plan ahead and spot meaningful change.

  • ForecastingProject demand or revenue with validated intervals using AutoARIMA, AutoETS, Theta, drift, or seasonal baselines.
  • Anomaly detectionFlag unusual periods against a seasonal or trend expectation with MSTL, LOWESS, or 3-sigma.
  • Changepoint detectionFind structural shifts in a trend with PELT.
  • Seasonality analysisSeparate trend, recurring cycles, and remainder with MSTL or STL.
Explain and quantify

Understand what changed and what predicts it.

  • Contribution analysisDecompose a period-to-period change across business dimensions.
  • CorrelationMeasure how two metrics move together, including lagged relationships, with Pearson and Granger tests.
  • Driver analysisRank predictive factors with gradient boosting and held-out permutation importance.
  • RegressionEstimate interpretable relationships with OLS coefficients, intervals, and fit metrics.
Segment and evaluate

Compare groups, risks, retention, and outcomes.

  • ClusteringDiscover groups of similar customers, products, or stores with K-means or HDBSCAN.
  • ClassificationEstimate a binary likelihood such as churn risk with interpretable logistic regression.
  • Cohort retentionTrack how activity changes across signup cohorts and later periods.
  • A/B testingCompare two variants with confidence intervals and a proportion z-test or Welch's t-test.

Supported methods depend on deployment configuration and data suitability. Correlation and predictive importance show association—not proof of causation.

FAQ

What teams ask before they connect their data.

How do Jeen Insights and Jeen Data work together?

Jeen Data is the semantic layer and curated catalog. Jeen Insights is the natural-language analytics workspace that uses that catalog to query registered sources and show the evidence behind each answer.

Can Jeen Insights change source data?

No. Data-source execution is read-only, and generated work passes the configured governance and validation checks before it can run.

What context does the model receive?

The active connection's curated catalog can include tables, columns, relationships, business terms, profiles, and knowledge pairs, together with the conversation context needed for the question.

Does Jeen Insights optimize inventory?

Jeen Insights can forecast demand by retailer or another business dimension and show the supporting intervals, trends, and model evidence. It provides forecast-informed decision support; your planning team applies lead times, service levels, costs, and operational constraints to set inventory policy.

Where can it run?

Jeen Insights is designed for deployment in your environment, including cloud, on-premises, and isolated enterprise configurations.
