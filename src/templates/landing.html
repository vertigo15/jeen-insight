Jeen Data semantic layer

Grounded in the definitions your business trusts.

Jeen Data, curated through Schema Modeler, supplies the context for every registered connection: tables, columns, relationships, business terms, profiles, and knowledge pairs.

Jeen Insights loads that catalog before it plans a query or analysis. The model starts with your definitions—not a generic interpretation of the warehouse.

  • Curated once and shared across the Jeen platform
  • Scoped to the active connection at question time
  • Inspectable and correctable by your data team

FAQ

What teams ask before they connect their data.

How do Jeen Insights and Jeen Data work together?

Jeen Data is the semantic layer and curated catalog. Jeen Insights is the natural-language analytics workspace that uses that catalog to query registered sources and show the evidence behind each answer.

Can Jeen Insights change source data?

No. Data-source execution is read-only, and generated work passes the configured governance and validation checks before it can run.

What context does the model receive?

The active connection's curated catalog can include tables, columns, relationships, business terms, profiles, and knowledge pairs, together with the conversation context needed for the question.

Where can it run?

Jeen Insights is designed for deployment in your environment, including cloud, on-premises, and isolated enterprise configurations.
