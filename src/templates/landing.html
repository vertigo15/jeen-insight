HOW IT WORKS

Four steps between a question and a defensible answer.

01
Ask in plain language
A router classifies the question — answerable from memory, needs a query, out of scope, or unsafe.
02
Curated metadata to a plan
Tables, columns, relationships and business terms for that connection go into the prompt, and the agent picks its tool: a query, a DAX measure, or Python analytics.
03
Governed execution
Nothing runs until it has passed your governance policy, and it runs with read-only access to the source.
04
Chart, rows, insight
An eval node checks the result against intent, writes a summary and key findings, and proposes follow-up questions.

FAQ
What does it know about our data?
Only the metadata your team curates for each connection. That grounding is inspectable and editable, so a wrong answer is traceable to a definition you can fix.
Can it change our data?
No. Access to every connection is read-only, and anything the agent generates is checked against your governance policy before it is allowed to run.
Which models can we use?
Whichever your tenant approves. The model provider is a deployment setting, and the agent is not tied to one vendor.
How does it learn our business terms?
Business terms and question/answer knowledge pairs are curated per connection and travel with the metadata bundle the agent loads.
Where does it run?
In your environment. Docker Compose for local and a Helm chart for AKS, with prompts editable from Settings without a redeploy.
